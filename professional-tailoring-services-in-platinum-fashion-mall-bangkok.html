Tailoring in Platinum Fashion Mall

G.Armani Suit is an independent bespoke tailoring shop on the 4th floor of Platinum Fashion Mall in Pratunam, Bangkok. A suit typically takes 3 to 5 days over two to three fittings.

The shop makes suits, wedding and black-tie wear, business suits, shirts and alterations for men.
